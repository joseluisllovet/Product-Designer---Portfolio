About me

Complex workflows, made clear and consistent.

I get into the problem before I touch Figma. With a Master’s in Interaction & UX Design and a background in advertising, I balance user needs, business goals and technical constraints, from early discovery to implementation-ready handoff. At Formalize I designed compliance tools used by over 5 million employees; at AeroGuest, hotel operations and digital keys for more than a million guests across the Nordics and the UK.

How I work

01

Problem first

I start with the problem, not the pixels. At AeroGuest I lead UX for key product areas, from problem framing to shipped feature, using Shape Up to scope the work and usability tests to check it with real users. See selected work.

02

Built to scale

I’ve built three design systems from the ground up, including Formalize’s first, in Figma, and AeroGuest’s, which now supports all three of its products. Tokens, components, patterns and documentation that make implementation faster and more consistent.

03

Close to code

Before product design I built 20+ websites in HTML, CSS, JavaScript and WordPress, so I know how things get made. I partner daily with PMs and engineers and hand off designs that are ready to build.
